# Emil Design Eng

Use this skill when the user wants a screen, page, or flow to feel polished, restrained, and high-end.

## When To Use

- The UI needs to feel elite rather than merely functional.
- You are reviewing craft, motion, spacing, hierarchy, or visual rhythm.
- The work needs the "5c" level polish pass from the design workflow.
- The user says things like sexy UI, visually stunning, anti-slop, craft check, or motion review.

## Core Approach

1. Start with restraint.
   - Prefer fewer, stronger visual moves over decorative noise.
   - Use calm hierarchy, clear spacing, and one dominant accent direction.

2. Design for trust.
   - Every surface should feel deliberate, editable, and understandable.
   - No gimmicks, no novelty for its own sake.

3. Polish the motion last.
   - Motion should support intent, not announce itself.
   - Keep animations short, interruptible, and subtle.

4. Audit the details.
   - Typography scale
   - alignment and spacing
   - component consistency
   - hover / focus / empty / loading / error states
   - visual density
   - brand voice in copy

## Rules

- Use baseline UI first, then polish, then high-craft only where the surface deserves it.
- Keep motion under 300ms.
- Prefer transform and opacity.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid animation on high-frequency surfaces and keyboard actions.
- Hover effects should be gated to hover-capable pointers.
- Do not add visual noise just to make a page feel more "designed."

## What To Look For

- Does the page have one clear focal point?
- Does the spacing feel intentional?
- Does the color system feel coherent and adult?
- Do cards, buttons, and panels share one visual language?
- Are states clear without explaining themselves?
- Does the page feel premium at a glance and calm on second look?

## Output Style

When reviewing, respond with:
- what feels weak
- what should change
- what should stay
- the smallest set of edits that materially improves the experience

## Guardrails

- Do not over-animate.
- Do not rely on generic SaaS gradients.
- Do not collapse every page into the same safe pattern.
- Do not change the visual system without a reason tied to the product.
